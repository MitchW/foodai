Fud AI Scan Lab

Runs this repo's exact food-image prompt and pipeline against a photo, in the browser. Built to answer one question: with no context, just an image, how accurate is it and what can it actually scan? Log Bitebro's answer and the true value beside each scan, then export the whole comparison as CSV.

1 · AI provider (bring your own key)

Stored only in this browser's localStorage and sent straight to the provider — nothing else sees it. Still: this is a local test harness, so use a throwaway/limited key, not a production one. Gemini is the most reliable choice from a browser; OpenAI and Anthropic may be blocked by CORS depending on your setup.

Model presets in the app: gemini-3.5-flash-lite (the app's own default — cheapest), gemini-3.5-flash, gemini-3.1-pro-preview, gpt-5.4-mini, claude-sonnet-5. Swap models and re-scan the same photo to see how much of "accuracy" is really just model choice.

2 · Snap or pick a photo

Downscaled to 1600px longest side at JPEG quality 0.8 — identical to encodedJPEGData in the iOS app, so results match what the shipped app would send.
